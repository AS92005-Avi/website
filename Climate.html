What is Climate Change?

image

Climate change refers to long-term changes in Earth's temperature and weather patterns. While some changes occur naturally, human activities such as burning fossil fuels have greatly increased greenhouse gas emissions.

Who does it affect?

image

Climate change can affect a whole range of people. But specifically, it impacts pacific islands. The peoples homes are being destroyed by rising sea levels, extreme weather and freshwater/soil salinisation. The people of those pacific islands are forced to relocate away from the land they hold close to their heart. This is extremely unfair for them considering they arent the ones polluting the world.
