Ways you can help :

Using less electricity helps reduce greenhouse gas emissions. Simple actions such as turning off lights, unplugging devices, and using energy-efficient appliances can make a difference.

Reducing waste helps conserve resources and decreases pollution. Reusing items and recycling materials can reduce the amount of rubbish sent to landfills.

Walking, cycling, carpooling, or using public transport can help reduce emissions from vehicles and improve air quality.

Trees absorb carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and provide habitats for wildlife. Participating in local tree-planting projects can help restore natural environments.
